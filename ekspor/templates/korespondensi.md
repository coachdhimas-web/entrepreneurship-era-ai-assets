## TIPS UNTUK COACH

### Best Practices Email Ekspor:

1. **Subject Line**
   - Keep it short (under 50 characters)
   - Include product/category keyword
   - Avoid spam triggers (FREE, URGENT, !!!)
   - Personalize when possible

2. **Timing**
   - Send Tuesday-Thursday, morning (target market timezone)
   - Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
   - Consider recipient's timezone

3. **Format**
   - Plain text is often better than HTML
   - Use bullet points for readability
   - Include all key information in first paragraph
   - Use signature with full contact details

4. **Follow-up Etiquette**
   - Wait 2-3 business days before follow-up
   - Max 3-4 follow-ups before moving to cold list
   - Track all communications in CRM

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- Spelling/grammar errors (use grammar checker)
- Too salesy or pushy language
- Missing clear call-to-action
- Over-attaching files (keep under 5MB total)
- Sending without personalization
